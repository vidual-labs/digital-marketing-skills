# TikTok Ads Creatives & Copy

## Overview

Create native-feeling creative concepts, scripts and ad text for TikTok advertising. TikTok rewards content that looks like it belongs in the For You feed: vertical, fast, unpolished, hook-first. This skill covers hook formulas, timed script structures, the small set of in-app text fields, format and campaign-type choices (including Spark Ads and Smart+), and a testing plan that isolates one variable at a time.

## When to Use

- Writing TikTok ad scripts and concepts from an offer or landing page
- Briefing creators for UGC or Spark Ads
- Adapting Meta, YouTube or TV creative so it survives on TikTok
- Producing hook variations for creative testing
- Writing ad text, display names and CTA choices for TikTok Ads Manager

Don't use for: organic TikTok content strategy, TikTok Shop catalog or seller logistics, influencer contract negotiation, or diagnosing campaign metrics (apply the structure of `meta-ads-diagnostics` with TikTok's metrics instead).

## Inputs

Ask for (or extract from the conversation):

- **Offer**: product/service, price point, the one thing it changes for the buyer
- **Audience**: age range, interests, the moment they would use the product, their slang level
- **Funnel stage** and campaign objective (reach, traffic, lead gen, app, conversions, TikTok Shop sales)
- **Proof** that can be shown on camera (results, reviews, demos) — never invented
- **Production reality**: who can film (founder, team, creators), budget for UGC, existing footage
- **Landing destination**: website, instant form, app store, TikTok Shop
- **Markets and languages** — hooks and slang are locale-specific

Data rules: do not fabricate testimonials or statistics; write `[insert proof]` and say what is missing. Keep claims inside TikTok's ad policies (no exaggerated before/after, no health or finance claims without substantiation, age-gated categories flagged). Field names and limits below reflect Ads Manager in 2026; if the UI differs, follow the UI and mention it.

## TikTok In-App Ad Fields (2026)

| Field | Shows | Limit | Job |
|-------|-------|-------|-----|
| **Ad text** (caption) | Below the display name, over the video | 1–100 characters; ~40 visible before "more" on many devices; no emojis in some markets | One-line hook restatement or offer; the video carries the message |
| **Display name** | Above the ad text | 2–20 characters (Latin scripts) | Brand or product name |
| **CTA button** | Bottom of screen | Fixed list (Shop now, Learn more, Sign up, Download, Contact us, Book now, Get quote, Apply now, Order now, …) or "Dynamic" | Match the destination |
| **Interactive add-ons** | Overlay cards, countdowns, gift codes, polls | Optional | Lift CTR on conversion campaigns |
| **Video** | Full screen, 9:16 | 5–60 s for most placements (ads up to 10 min allowed, rarely useful) | The entire argument |
| **Carousel / image cards** | Swipeable images | 2–35 images, each with optional text | Catalog and multi-angle products |

There is no separate headline/description pair for standard in-feed video ads; carousel and catalog formats add per-card text. The video and its on-screen text do the work copy does on other platforms.

## Creative Philosophy: Native First

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Shoot vertical 9:16 on a phone, handheld feel | Repurpose 16:9 or over-lit studio footage |
| Open on action, a face mid-sentence, or a bold on-screen line | Open with a logo, intro card or slow establishing shot |
| Talk like a person: contractions, pauses, imperfect takes | Read a brand voiceover |
| Use TikTok-native text styles, captions and a trending or original sound | Add agency motion graphics and stock music |
| Show the product in a real place, in real hands | White-background product shots |
| Hook in 1–2 seconds | Spend 3 seconds "setting the scene" |

Keep the top ~130 px and bottom ~35% of the frame (caption, buttons, sound row) free of essential text.

## Hook Formulas

Users decide in roughly 1–2 seconds. The hook is both the first spoken line and the first on-screen line.

1. **Statement**: "The biggest mistake [audience] makes with [topic]." / "Stop [common action]. Do this."
2. **Curiosity**: "I tried [thing] for [time] and [unexpected result]." / "Nobody talks about this [category] problem."
3. **Result**: "How I [specific result] in [timeframe]." / "[Number] people use [thing] wrong — here's the fix."
4. **Demo**: the product doing something impressive in frame 1, no words needed.
5. **Story**: "So I [did something] and [what happened]." / "My [friend/client] couldn't [problem] until…"
6. **POV / situational**: "POV: you finally [desired state]." — dates fast, verify the format is still current.
7. **Pattern interrupt**: odd angle, whip zoom, unexpected object; visual weirdness buys the second sentence.
8. **Comment reply / stitch style**: "Replying to @user: yes, here's how…" — feels like community, not an ad.

Hook rules: no greeting, no self-introduction, on-screen text mirrors the spoken hook, and the hook must still work muted.

## Script Structures

### 9–15 seconds (awareness, Spark Ads, top of funnel)

```
0–2 s   HOOK — spoken + on-screen + visual action
2–5 s   PROBLEM — one sentence, relatable
5–11 s  SOLUTION — product in use, one benefit shown not told
11–15 s CTA — spoken + on-screen; matches the button
```

### 20–30 seconds (consideration and most conversion ads)

```
0–2 s    HOOK
2–7 s    PROBLEM — specific, first person
7–12 s   AGITATE — what it costs them (time, money, embarrassment)
12–20 s  SOLUTION — demo or transformation
20–26 s  PROOF — "[n] people…", a review on screen, a result
26–30 s  CTA — urgency only if real
```

### 45–60 seconds (complex offers, high price, retargeting)

```
0–2 s    HOOK
2–10 s   STORY — the before state
10–20 s  SOLUTION — step-by-step demo
20–35 s  PROOF — testimonial voiceover, before/after (policy-safe), numbers
35–45 s  OBJECTION — the #1 reason people don't buy, answered
45–60 s  CTA + URGENCY — real deadline, real reason
```

Retention tip: add a visual change (cut, zoom, text pop, location) every 1–3 seconds; TikTok's own data ties cut frequency to completion rate.

## Formats and Campaign Types

| Format / type | What it is | Best for |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| **In-Feed video** | Auction ad in the For You feed | Default for every objective |
| **Spark Ads** | Boosts an organic post from your or a creator's account; keeps likes, comments, shares and profile link | UGC, creator partnerships, social proof; usually outperforms dark posts of the same video |
| **Smart+** | Automated campaign type (targeting, bidding, creative selection) similar to Meta Advantage+ | Accounts with ≥ 50 conversions/week and a steady creative supply |
| **Search Ads** | Ads in TikTok search results, keyword-targeted | High-intent queries, brand defence |
| **Carousel / image ads** | Swipeable images | Catalogs, multi-feature products |
| **Video Shopping Ads / GMV Max** | Linked to TikTok Shop products, automated | TikTok Shop sellers |
| **TopView** | First ad on app open | Launches, mass awareness (reservation buys) |
| **Branded Mission / Hashtag Challenge / Effects** | Creator-sourced UGC at scale | Large brand budgets |

Creator sourcing: TikTok One (creator marketplace) and Symphony (AI creative tools) exist inside the ads ecosystem; brief creators on hook and proof, not on a word-for-word script.

## CTA by Objective

| Objective | CTA |
|-----------|-----|
| Reach / video views | None or Learn more |
| Traffic / consideration | Learn more, View more |
| Lead generation (instant form) | Sign up, Get quote, Apply now |
| Conversions / e-commerce | Shop now, Order now, Get offer |
| App installs | Download, Install now |
| TikTok Shop | Shop now (product anchor) |

## Testing Strategy

| Test | Change | Keep constant | Metric |
|------|--------|---------------|--------|
| Hook | First 2 seconds only | Body, CTA, sound | 2-second view rate, 6-second view rate |
| Length | 15 s vs 30 s cut of one concept | Hook, offer | Completion rate, CPA |
| Style | UGC vs brand-produced | Script | CTR, CPA |
| Sound | Trending vs original vs VO | Visuals | 6-second view rate |
| CTA | Button + spoken CTA | Creative | CTR, CVR |
| Spark vs dark | Same video via creator account vs brand | Everything | CPM, CTR, CPA |

Use the TikTok Creative Center (Top Ads, Keyword Insights, Trend Discovery) to check what is working in the category before filming, and check that a "trending" sound is licensed for commercial use in Ads Manager's Commercial Music Library.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Porting Meta creative unchanged.** Too polished, too long, too "ad". Re-shoot vertical and native.

2. **Hook arrives at second 3.** The scroll already happened. Hook in frame 1.

3. **No captions or on-screen text.** A large share of views are muted. No text, no message.

4. **Over-production.** iPhone footage with jump cuts routinely beats agency films here. Spend on more concepts, not more polish.

5. **Ignoring Spark Ads.** Running a creator's video as a dark post throws away the social proof and the creator's profile credibility.

6. **Writing a 2,000-character caption.** Ad text is 100 characters and mostly hidden. Put the argument in the video.

7. **Using unlicensed sounds.** Organic trending sounds are often not cleared for ads. Check the Commercial Music Library or use original audio.

8. **One hook across ten ads.** TikTok's system detects near-duplicates and throttles them. Rotate hooks weekly.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 creatives with distinct hook types and a stated hypothesis each
- [ ] Every hook lands in the first 2 seconds, spoken and on screen, and works muted
- [ ] Scripts are timestamped with spoken line, on-screen text and visual direction per segment
- [ ] Ad text ≤ 100 characters with the key words in the first 40; display name ≤ 20 characters
- [ ] CTA matches the objective and destination
- [ ] Safe zones respected (no essential text in the top ~130 px or bottom third)
- [ ] Sound choice is commercially licensed or original
- [ ] Campaign type chosen deliberately (In-Feed, Spark, Smart+, Search) with a reason
- [ ] Proof is real or marked `[insert proof]`; claims are policy-safe
- [ ] Pinned comment strategy included
- [ ] Testing plan isolates one variable per test with a named metric
